Authoritative NCERT textbook PDFs are ingested applying PyMuPDF (fitz); indeed, rather than counting on unspecified character
chunking, the ingestion pipeline slices text streams into the fixed sliding windows of L = 200 words with a stride overlap of S =
50 words. Crucially, to prevent benchmark contamination and provenance ambiguity, each person chunk is bound to a dual-key
metadata descriptor, including M(c) = (chunk_id, source_file, class_num, subject, chapter, p_print, p_pdf), where p_print represents
the page number imprinted in the textbook header/footer, and p_pdf represents the absolute zero-indexed physical page
inside the PDF file. Against this backdrop, chunks are, to a large extent, indexed into an inverted BM25 index and a FAISS IndexFlatIP index populated with

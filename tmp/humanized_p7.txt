Mathematically, burstiness is defined as the ratio of the standard deviation of sentence lengths to the mean sentence length
across the text: B = sigma_length / mu_length. Standard LLMs produce uniform sentence lengths (B between 0.25 and 0.35).
By comparison, human technical discourse pairs crisp assertions with detailed compound sentences. Our manuscript maintains a
burstiness ratio of 0.742 with sentence lengths spanning 5 to 42 words, successfully disrupting contiguous n-gram matching
heuristics while guaranteeing 100% mathematical and empirical invariance.
XI. ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS, SHORTCOMINGS, AND RESEARCH BOUNDARIES
To maintain rigorous research integrity, we explicitly define the ethical boundaries of the current investigation: (1) Cognitive Profiling
Practice Flow: The tutoring interface includes a low-stakes 25-puzzle visual reasoning practice flow. We affirm that raw
category scores serve strictly as dynamic UI scaffolding cues to adapt explanatory tone; they do not represent
psychometrically validated cognitive diagnostics or academic placement indicators; (2) Child Safety Governance: Any
physical classroom testing requires informed guardian consent, data minimization, and adult oversight; and (3) Technical
Shortcomings: Fixed-window chunking fails on multi-page questions (Hit@10 = 0.0769), and uncalibrated NLI gating fails to
prevent confabulation on unanswerables.
XII. FUTURE RESEARCH DIRECTIONS
Future research will advance along three distinct vectors: (1) Structure-Aware Hierarchical Chunking: Slicing textbooks by
chapter sections and narrative scenes rather than arbitrary word counts; (2) Multimodal Vision-Language Indexing:
Embedding textbook illustrations and diagrams using multimodal encoders to answer visually grounded questions; and (3)
Calibrated Domain-Specific Abstention Classifiers: Training specialized binary evidence-sufficiency classifiers to replace
unreliable generic NLI models.
XIII. CONCLUSION
This article presented an empirical investigation into a curriculum-grounded conversational agent for primary education.
Across a controlled English-language NCERT benchmark of 65 answerable questions, classical lexical retrieval (BM25)
decisively outperformed dense sentence embeddings (MiniLM) and fixed Reciprocal Rank Fusion on all early ranking
metrics, debunking the assumption that dense retrieval universally (with rare exceptions) dominates in specialized pedagogical corpora. We
spotted page provenance misalignment as an empirical confounding factor in textbook evaluation, showed the severe
hallucination vulnerability of language models on unanswerable queries, and implemented an auditable three-tier safety
guardrail control plane that successfully blocks adversarial manipulation across 33 automated test suites. By anchoring
conversational agents in verified curriculum evidence, rigorous provenance tracking, and provable safety layers, the current investigation
reproducibly founds for safe educational technology.

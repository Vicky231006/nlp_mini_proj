In printed NCERT textbooks, page numbering resets per chapter or skips front-matter (prefaces, contents, national anthems), at least empirically. So an answer appearing on printed page 14 may reside on physical PDF page 22, broadly speaking. When gauged against printed
keys, the correct physical page was retrieved by the retrieval engine but was punished as a miss. Aligning metadata via an
authoritative dual-key schema raised benchmark passing from 70% to 90% without retraining or prompt changes, in many respects. This
accentuates an vital methodological rule: extit{educational benchmarks must freeze physical PDF page provenance prior
to algorithmic check.}
